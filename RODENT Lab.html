What to try with the class

1. Compare the energy barsRun "Normal day" for a minute. Surveillance data goes over LoRa or BLE in RODENT, so the nodes spend far less energy than on WiFi.
2. Turn on an alarm at N4RODENT switches that node to WiFi for alarm messages, so alarm delay drops to WiFi level while surveillance stays cheap.
3. Look at N5N5 has no WiFi. The classic protocol cannot reach it at all. RODENT relays through N4 and picks WiFi or LoRa beyond N4 per message type.
4. Break the WiFi baseThe classic network goes silent. RODENT detects the dead link, drops the routes and moves everything to LoRa.
